USAGE FROM AGENTFORCE SKILL
----------------------------
Action Name: "Get Account Context"
Category: Sales Assistant
Input: recordId (Account/Contact/Opportunity ID) OR accountName (string)
Output: contextJson (comprehensive account brief in JSON)

Example prompts:
- "Get account brief for DEMO Lakeshore Medical Group"
- "Show me the account context for Lakeshore"
